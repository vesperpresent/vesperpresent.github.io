Questions

Good questions.

Anything else? Open Vespa and look around. It is free.

Is Vespa really free?

Yes. Vespa is free for every church. There are no paid tiers, no watermarks and no credit card.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Vespa runs in a modern browser like Chrome or Edge. Open it on the computer connected to your projector or TV.

How do I show slides on a projector or second screen?

Click Present. Vespa opens a separate presentation window. In Chrome and Edge it can place itself on your second screen; otherwise drag it there and double-click for full screen.

Can I control Vespa from my phone?

Yes. Scan the QR code in Vespa and sign in with the same account. Your phone becomes a remote with next, back, black and logo buttons.

Does Vespa include Bible verses?

Yes. Type a reference like John 3:16-18 and Vespa builds the slides for you in several public domain translations, including the World English Bible and King James Version.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. Choose Continue with Google on the sign-in page, or create an account with any email address.

Is my account secure?

Every account verifies its email address, and you can turn on two-step verification and sign out other devices from Settings.

Vespa

Vespa is free presentation software for churches. It puts song lyrics, sermons, Bible verses, photos and videos on the screens in your church. It runs in your web browser, so there is nothing to install.

Admin login

For the person who prepares the service. Add songs, sermons, scripture, photos and videos, and put them in order.

Projector login

For the person at the computer during the service. Control the front screen and the back screen.

Both logins can use an email address and password, or a Google account.

What you can show

  • Songs. Paste the lyrics and Vespa splits them into slides. Name parts like Verse 1 and Chorus.
  • Sermons. Type your points and insert Bible verses as you go.
  • Scripture. Type a reference such as John 3:16-18 and the slides are made for you, in several public domain translations.
  • Photos. One picture or a set of pictures.
  • Videos. Announcements and countdowns, with or without sound.
  • Slides. Notices and anything else made of text.

Two screens

The front screen is what the church sees on the projector or TV. The back screen is for the people on stage. It can show the current words, the next slide, the time, a countdown and private messages, or simply copy the front screen.

The projector person can black the screen, show your church logo, hide the words, and put a short message on either screen. Arrow keys and presentation clickers work, and a phone can be used as a remote.

How to start

  1. Create an admin account and confirm your email address.
  2. Add your songs and other items, then make a service and put them in order.
  3. In the admin Settings, type the email address of your projector person.
  4. The projector person creates a projector account with that email address and opens the projector page on the computer connected to the screens.

Questions

Is Vespa free?
Yes. Vespa is free for every church. There are no paid plans and no credit card.
Do I need to install anything?
No. Use a web browser such as Chrome or Edge. Chrome and Edge can also open the front screen on your second display by themselves.
Why are there two logins?
So the person running the screens cannot change or delete your songs and sermons by accident. The admin prepares, the projector person presents.
Is my account safe?
Every account must confirm its email address. You can also turn on two-step sign in and sign out of other devices.
